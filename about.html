About the project

An honest, independently tested reference

CPSC is a vendor-neutral reference for physical security. It is a University of Minnesota faculty project, assembled from the people who install and live with this hardware, and checked in a lab against the same attacks it documents.

How it is assembled
Three inputs, in order

Nothing here is a product brochure. Every entry starts with the field and ends in a lab.

1

Listen

The weaknesses and the fixes come from the people who work these doors every day: integrators who install the hardware, and the security teams, locksmiths, and facility managers who have to live with it.

2

Verify

Every technical claim, product, and code citation is checked against primary sources, manufacturer documentation, and the relevant standards before it goes on the page. When something cannot be confirmed, the page says so.

3

Test

Where we can get hardware in hand, we run it in the lab against the real attacks: compressed-gas spoofing, under-door tools, shimming, and the rest. The result decides whether it stays on the site.

The lab
Send us a unit

The offer to vendors is simple and the terms are fixed.

Free, honest testing for demo hardware

Ship a demo product to our lab and we will test it against the common attacks documented on this site, at no cost. The write-up is public either way. A vendor cannot buy a recommendation here, only earn one.

If it does not hold up, we simply remove it as a recommendation and explain why. There is no penalty and no drama; the point is an accurate site.

If it performs, we recommend it, highlight it, and publish short video showing exactly how it resists the attack, so an integrator or end-user can see the evidence rather than take our word for it.

No pay-to-play. Money never buys placement, a rating, or a recommendation.

Results are public. Good or bad, the test outcome is posted, with the method described.

Claims are scoped. No device is unbeatable; we say what an item resists and what it does not.
