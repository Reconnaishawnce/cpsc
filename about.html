About the project

An honest, independent reference

CPSC is a vendor-neutral reference for physical security professionals. It is managed by Shawn Abelson, and assembled from the people who install and live with this hardware, then checked against primary sources and the relevant standards.

Independent Vendor-neutral Open access Practitioner-informed
How it is built
From the field to the page

Nothing here is a product brochure. Each entry starts with practitioners and ends against primary sources.

1

Listen

The weaknesses and the fixes come from the people who work these openings every day: integrators who install the hardware, and the security teams, locksmiths, and facility managers who have to live with it.

2

Verify

Every technical claim, product, and code citation is checked against primary sources, manufacturer documentation, and the relevant standards before it goes on the page. When something cannot be confirmed, the page says so.

3

Maintain

Corrections and new field notes are folded back in, and entries are revised as products, standards, and the techniques against them change. The site is meant to stay current, not to be published once.

The ground rules
How recommendations work

The list is small and the rules are fixed, so a reader can trust why something is on it.

A product is listed because it addresses a documented weakness by design, and because its behavior can be verified from the manufacturer’s own specifications. No manufacturer sponsors this site, and placement cannot be bought. Recommendations are also scoped: no device is unbeatable, so each entry says what an item resists and what it does not.

Vendor-neutral. No sponsors, and money never buys placement or a recommendation.

Claims are scoped. Each item says what it resists and what it does not; nothing is called unbeatable.

Corrections win. A sourced correction changes the page, and a rec that no longer holds up comes off it.
